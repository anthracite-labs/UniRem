Living Room Noir

A dark-room instrument, not an app: true-black OLED surfaces disappear against the living room, warm ivory type sits at the edge of readability so the TV stays the brightest thing you look at, and the only saturated color in the entire product is the ambient state ring telling you the truth about your last keypress. Density is deliberately low, type is light-weight, and motion is restricted to one slow breath of the ring per press — premium, quiet, cinematic.

  • Hero mode shown: (a) thumb-arc — D-pad in the bottom third. Mode (b) big-pad exists as the alternate hero.
  • Ring states depicted: Phone 1 — soft pulse (submitted) · Phone 2 — red + offline banner. Full five-state legend below.
  • Secondary screen: cast session ("Casting Grandma-80th.mp4…").
  • State variant: offline banner ("TV didn't answer — retry?").
  • Type weight / density / motion: 300–400 light · low density · single slow ring breath, 400ms settle, nothing else moves.
