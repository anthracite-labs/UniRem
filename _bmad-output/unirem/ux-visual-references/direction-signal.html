UniRem · UX Direction 4 of 4

Signal Brutalist

Built for eyes that are on the TV: pure black/white at 4px outlines, type at 900 weight, targets so large they can be hit from peripheral vision one-handed in the dark. There is no decoration, no radius, no gradient, and no motion — states cut instantly and every ring state is also written in words, because a color should never be the only messenger. Three hues exist in the entire product, and all three belong to the ring.

  • Hero mode shown: (b) big-pad — oversized swipe slab (swipe = D-pad, tap = OK) with floating function-key row. Mode (a) thumb-arc exists as the alternate hero.
  • Ring states depicted: Phone 1 — idle (full white outline) · Phone 2 — red + offline banner. Legend shows all five.
  • Secondary screen: TVs management — empty state.
  • State variant: empty state ("No TVs yet — Add your first TV") + offline banner.
  • Motion implication: none. The only permitted animation is a 1Hz hard blink while unconfirmed.
